Delete your Hisab account

You can delete your account and all of its data at any time — inside the app under Settings, or right here.

Request deletion by email

Send a deletion request from the email address linked to your Hisab account. We verify ownership, then permanently erase your profile, transactions, plans, budgets, bills, goals, digests, and notification tokens — usually within 7 days.

Request deletion
What gets deleted

Everything stored under your account: sign-in profile, transactions, morning plans, evening digests, budgets, bills, savings goals, consent log, and push tokens. Backups, if any, age out within 30 days. Deletion is irreversible.

Prefer the app? Open Hisab → Settings → Delete account.
